Valmont Custom Homes · Twelve designs

The Homes

Rooted in Diamond Valley

The Valmont Custom Home Collection.

Twelve designs: seven single-level bungalows and five two-storey plans, from 1,100 to 2,990 sq ft. Every model carries the name of an animal raised right here in Diamond Valley on the ranch of our community's developer, Don Beaton: eleven of his horses, and one much-loved donkey. It's a small tribute to the land this community is built on, and the western heritage that still runs through it.

Valmont is a design-led builder: every plan, elevation, and finish is developed by their own in-house team rather than borrowed from a catalogue. Open any design for its exterior elevations, interior renderings, floor plans, standard features, and a downloadable brochure.

A note on square footage & pricing

The square footages listed for each design reflect the plans as drawn, and are accurate for planning and comparison purposes. Final square footage will be subject to the selected lot and its applicable requirements — including lot dimensions, grade and walkout conditions, setbacks, and any architectural or municipal guidelines that apply to that homesite. Starting prices include GST, are for the home only, and are subject to change without notice; renderings are artist's concept, and finishes and specifications may change.
