Valmont Custom Homes · Four designs

The Homes

Rooted in Diamond Valley

The Valmont Custom Home Collection.

Every model at Valmont Custom Homes carries the name of a horse raised right here in Diamond Valley, on the ranch of our community's developer, Don Beaton — a small tribute to the land this community is built on, and the western heritage that still runs through it.

Valmont is a design-led builder: every plan, elevation, and finish is developed by their own in-house team rather than borrowed from a catalogue. Each design below comes with a choice of exterior elevations, interior renderings, a full floor plan, and a downloadable brochure.

A note on square footage & pricing

The square footages listed for each design reflect the plans as drawn, and are accurate for planning and comparison purposes. Final square footage will be subject to the selected lot and its applicable requirements — including lot dimensions, grade and walkout conditions, setbacks, and any architectural or municipal guidelines that apply to that homesite. Starting prices are for the home only and are subject to change without notice; renderings are artist's concept, and finishes and specifications may change.
