Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information diamondridgeliving.ca collects, how we use and share it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Who we are

Diamond Ridge is a residential land development in Diamond Valley, Alberta. This website and the marketing for Diamond Ridge are operated by Root9 Marketing Ltd. on behalf of the developer. In this policy, “we,” “us,” and “our” mean Root9 Marketing Ltd., which is responsible for the personal information collected through this site.

Information you give us

We only collect personal information that you choose to send us through the site’s two forms:

  • Register your interest: your first and last name, email address, phone number, what you’re interested in, and, if you add them, your timeline and a message.
  • Book a showing: your name, email address, phone number (optional), preferred date and time, what you’d like to see, and any notes you add.

If you reach a form from a link on a homesite or a home, the form may be filled in with that homesite or home for you. You can change or remove it before you send.

Information collected automatically

Basic technical information. Like any website, our hosting provider receives your IP address, browser and device type, the page you asked for, and the page you came from each time a page loads. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure.

Advertising measurement, only with your consent. If you accept cookies, the Meta Pixel records the pages you visit and the actions you take on the site, and Meta may match this activity to your Facebook or Instagram account. If you decline, the Meta Pixel never loads.

We don’t use any other analytics or advertising tools on this site.

Cookies and your choices

On your first visit, a banner asks whether you allow cookies. Nothing is tracked until you choose Accept: the Meta Pixel, and the cookies it sets, only load after you do. The site remembers your choice in your browser’s local storage so it doesn’t ask on every page. That record stays on your device and isn’t sent to us.

You can change your mind at any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser’s settings.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your registration or showing request, and to confirm your appointment.
  • To send you an email confirming that we received your request.
  • To follow up with you about the homesites, homes and builders you asked about.
  • To send you marketing emails, only if you have agreed to receive them.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, only if you accepted cookies.
  • To keep the site secure and working, including filtering out spam.

We do not sell your personal information to anyone.

Where your information is processed

The companies that host this site and deliver our emails may store and process information outside Canada, mainly in the United States. While it is there, it is subject to the laws of that country, including lawful access by its authorities.

How long we keep it

Form submissions reach us as emails; the website itself doesn’t keep a database of them. We keep your information for as long as you remain interested in Diamond Ridge or until you ask us to delete it. If you haven’t been in touch with us for more than three years, we may delete it from our systems.

How we protect it

The site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, our forms only accept submissions sent from this site’s own pages, and the confirmation email we send you never repeats the message you typed. Access to your information is limited to the people who need it to help you. No method of sending or storing information is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect it.

Links to other websites

This site links to other websites, such as Instagram, Century 21 and the Government of Canada. We don’t control those sites, and their own privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Your rights

Under Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Alberta’s Personal Information Protection Act, you have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, withdraw your consent to marketing at any time, ask us to delete it, and complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta. To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Marketing emails (CASL)

Under Canada’s Anti-Spam Legislation, we only send you marketing emails after you have given us your consent. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us at any time to be removed from our list.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed, and we’ll note any significant changes on the website.
