Diamond Valley, Alberta

Discover Diamond Valley

The town

A small town that earned its character.

Diamond Valley is a real town, not a bedroom community — tree-lined streets, independent coffee shops, a gallery, a brewery, and a Saturday farmers' market. Spots like Black Sheep Coffee, Bluerock Gallery, Hard Knox Brewery, Eau Claire Distillery, the Chuckwagon Café, and The Westwood anchor an ordinary day, all a few minutes from home.

See the town in photos

Life in Diamond Valley

The pace

Where the days last longer.

Neighbours wave. Walking paths thread through town and out toward the river. Small businesses sell things they made themselves. It's the kind of place people raise families on purpose — mornings that start slow, evenings that linger.

The foothills

Where the prairie meets the mountains.

Diamond Ridge sits where open prairie gives way to rolling foothills and, beyond them, the Rockies. The Sheep River runs nearby, with Bluerock Wildland and Kananaskis Country to the west. You don't drive to a view here — you step outside into it.
