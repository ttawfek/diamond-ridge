The teams

Four builders. One standard of care.

We weren't looking for volume. We were looking for teams whose work we'd be proud to live next to. Valmont brings a custom collection designed around this land. Woodmaster brings a new collection to Diamond Ridge. Albion brings decades of architectural rigour and a complete in-house team. Sen brings a boutique approach, with a curated selection of finishes for every home. Different studios, one conviction: a home should reflect the people inside it.

Valmont Custom Homes

Custom homes, drawn around the way you live.

Valmont builds from a curated Custom Home Collection of six designs created for these lots: three bungalows with the primary suite on the main floor, and three two-storey plans from 2,170 to 2,990 sq ft. Every design is offered with a choice of exterior elevations, so neighbouring homes never read the same way, and each plan can be tailored before the first shovel goes in the ground. Explore all six, from The Taco to The Metallica, with renderings, floor plans, and downloadable brochures.

Albion Homes

Crafted for living, designed for you.

A forty-year company history behind partners Grainger Nimmo, Jon Schofield, and Paige Winter. Grainger brings over 50 years in construction shaping Calgary's residential landscape; Jon is an award-winning architect with experience across Europe and North America; Paige ensures every homeowner's voice carries from design through completion. What sets Albion apart is a complete in-house team, with architecture, construction, and design under one roof. Three built homes on Phase 1, ready for possession.
